## Methodology

`bench.sh` starts the worker pools, runs 1 warmup + N hot conversions of the
same proof, and records: makespan per hot run, cores-busy (from `/proc/stat`
delta over the run), peak summed worker `RssAnon`, min `MemAvailable` seen, vk
hash match, OOM (watchdog kills at <700 MB avail), and the main + proof-systems
commit SHAs. Hardware: i7-13700H, 6 P-cores (HT) + 8 E-cores = 20 threads, 32 GB.

## Findings / dead ends

Settled conclusions, so we stop relitigating them on stale numbers:

- **K=8 / R5 / 4-4-4 is the scheduling optimum (~126–132s).** Sweeps of K,
  RAYON, pool count, sharding (contig vs striped), worker-centric dispatch,
  wrap-priority, and BASE_FIRST all land at or above this. K=9 OOMs.

- **Pinning / homogeneous core partitioning is WORSE (148–159s).** Forcing each
  prover onto only P-cores or only E-cores loses to plain global
  oversubscription — the OS scheduler launders the hybrid P/E heterogeneity
  better than any static affinity.

- **The quotient-gate (RAM-decoupling the d8 stage) does not help.** Bounding
  concurrent d8 lets utilization rise (19/20 cores at high in-flight) but OOMs
  there; the RAM-safe in-flight (~10) gives no makespan gain (~130–132s). The
  d8 transient was NOT the binding RAM constraint — per-proof working memory is.
  Reverted; never committed.

- **Fat R20 "tip" worker for the last 2 compression layers is WORSE (134–136s).**
  The merge circuits are small and don't scale past ~5 effective cores (R20
  thrashes), and a dedicated tip worker goes cold idling during the base phase.
  The tail is dependency-bound (waiting on children), not core-starved.

- **Not memory-bandwidth-bound.** perf shows ~8.8 GB/s (≈10% of peak), IPC 1.37.
  The underutilization is Amdahl (per-proof serial sections) + hybrid-CPU
  heterogeneity, not bandwidth.

- **~83% of the makespan is proving compute (MSM / quotient).** The only levers
  with real mass left are *work-reduction*, not scheduling: the MSM (batch-affine
  / GLV on the EC adds) and possibly the oracles witness-gen (limited — only the
  Fiat-Shamir challenges are redundant, and witness-gen overlaps proving).

### Open / in-flight

- **d8-free** (freeing the d8 witness+lookup evals right after the quotient,
  rather than at end-of-proof): lowers post-quotient d8 residency, but its
  explicit drops add munmap/re-fault churn. A/B in progress to decide whether
  it's a ~6s makespan cost (revert) or RAM-safety keeper. Committed in
  `981efdf638` (entangled with the parallelization/fresh-cache opts).
